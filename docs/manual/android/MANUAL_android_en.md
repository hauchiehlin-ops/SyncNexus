# SyncNexus User Manual — Android Edition

## 1. Overview & Highlights
**SyncNexus for Android** is a local-first, zero-cloud-lock-in real-time sync tool for Android smartphones and tablets. It syncs directly with computers over Wi-Fi without third-party servers.

* **Google Scoped Storage (SAF)**: Adheres to Google Play standards; only accesses folders you explicitly select.
* **Foreground Service**: Ensures background syncing continues uninterrupted while the screen is locked.
* **P2P LAN Connectivity**: Supports Android Network Service Discovery (NSD) to seamlessly locate Macs and PCs.

---

## 2. Quick Start: Adding Folders

Tap "**Add Folder**" on the main screen:

1. **Internal Storage**:
   - In the system document picker, tap the top-left menu icon, choose your phone name or "Internal Storage", navigate to your folder, and tap "Use this folder".
2. **External SD Card / USB OTG Drive**:
   - Plug in your SD card or Type-C USB drive. From the picker's sidebar, select your SD card or USB storage device, choose your folder, and tap "Use this folder".

---

## 3. Automatic Sync & Tips

* **LAN Synchronization**: When your phone and computers share the same Wi-Fi, SyncNexus connects automatically.
* **Battery Optimization**: For continuous background sync of large datasets, set SyncNexus battery usage to "Unrestricted" in Android Settings.
