# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: October 2, 2026  
**Application**: Sync-Nexus

Sync-Nexus is a local-first peer-to-peer file synchronization utility. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our principles regarding data collection and storage.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Usage
* **Zero Servers, No Accounts**: Sync-Nexus operates completely offline and peer-to-peer over local networks. We operate no cloud backend servers and require no user registration or accounts.
* **Zero Telemetry & Tracking**: The application contains no third-party analytics, metrics collection, or tracking SDKs.
* **No External Data Exfiltration**: Synchronization occurs strictly between local folders on your device or directly over your trusted local Wi-Fi network (P2P). Your file contents, filenames, and metadata never leave your local environment.

---

### 2. System Permissions
Sync-Nexus requests minimal permissions necessary for core operation:
* **User-Selected Folders & Files**: Used exclusively to read, monitor, and synchronize directories you deliberately choose.
* **Security-Scoped Bookmarks (macOS) / Storage Access Framework (Android)**: Persists authorized folder access across application restarts.
* **Removable Storage Volumes**: Used to detect and sync with connected USB external drives and SD cards.
* **Local Network (Bonjour / mDNS)**: Discovers nearby peer devices on the same Wi-Fi without internet connectivity.
